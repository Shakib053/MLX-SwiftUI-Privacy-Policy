MLX Chat

Privacy Policy

How the app handles chats, documents, photos, model downloads, and information you choose to share.

Effective date: October 26, 2026  ·  Publisher: Kazi Tanjim Shakib

At a glance. MLX Chat processes AI requests on your device. Chats and results you save are stored on your device. The app connects to Hugging Face to download an MLX model when you choose to do so; it does not send your prompts, conversations, imported content, or generated answers to Hugging Face for inference.

1. Scope

This policy covers the MLX Chat iOS app for iPhone and iPad. It describes the current app behavior. Separate services you open through a link or the iOS share sheet have their own privacy practices.

2. Information the app handles

  • Chats: Prompts, generated replies, conversation titles, timestamps, selected model, and token counts may be stored in the app’s local database.
  • Workspace content: Text you enter or import from a photo, camera image, PDF, TXT, or Markdown file is used to complete your chosen task. The app extracts text from images and reads documents on device. If you choose Save, it stores the source text and generated result locally.
  • Preferences: Onboarding, appearance, and model choices are stored on your device. Downloaded model files are cached on your device.
  • Feedback: The app prepares a suggested feedback template and opens the iOS share sheet. You choose the destination and what to send. The app does not silently transmit feedback.

You do not need an account to use the app. The current app code does not include an advertising SDK, app analytics SDK, or user tracking.

3. How AI processing works

On compatible devices, the app uses Apple Foundation Models or an MLX model downloaded to your device. Prompts, relevant chat history, imported text, and generated responses are processed locally. The current app has no hosted AI inference service. AI output can be inaccurate; review it before relying on it, especially for important decisions.

4. Network connections and third parties

If you download a model, the app connects to Hugging Face to obtain model files. Hugging Face and network providers may receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and the requested model, under their own policies. Model downloads do not require sending your chat or workspace content. Apple provides the operating system features used for local AI, camera and photo selection, file access, sharing, and device storage; Apple’s handling of device and service data is governed by its own policies.

If you tap Copy or Share, iOS or the destination you select may handle the content you choose. The app does not sell personal information or share app content with advertisers.

5. Storage, retention, and deletion

Conversations remain in the app’s local storage until you delete them or remove the app. Saved task results remain until you delete them or remove the app. Imported content that you do not save is used for the current task and is not intentionally kept as a saved task result. You can delete individual conversations and saved results in the app; removing a conversation also removes its stored messages. You can remove downloaded models from the Models screen. Uninstalling the app removes its local app data, subject to iOS backup and device settings outside the app’s control.

Because the app does not operate a chat account or chat server, there is no remote conversation store for us to delete. If you send feedback through another service, contact that service or recipient about its retained copy.

6. Your choices

  • Choose whether to import a photo, use the camera, or open a document. Camera access can be changed in iOS Settings.
  • Choose whether to save task results, download a model, copy content, share content, or send feedback.
  • Delete saved conversations and task results in the app, and remove downloaded models when no longer needed.

7. Security

Local data benefits from the security controls of iOS and your device. We do not claim that any device or network is completely secure. Use a device passcode and keep iOS updated. Avoid entering information you would not want stored on your device or included in a backup.

8. Children

The app is a general-purpose AI tool and is not designed specifically for children. Please contact us if you believe a child has sent personal information to us through feedback so we can review the request.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. We will update the effective date on this page. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate app or release notice.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Kazi Tanjim Shakib at nusratjahan.iosdev@gmail.com. Include enough detail for us to understand your request, but do not send private prompts or documents unless necessary.
